  Description:  The Copernicus Land Monitoring Service delivers pan-European and global land-related datasets including digital elevation models, land cover classification, vegetation, and water-related products.   Limitations:  Not a real-time or near-real-time monitoring system; Products updated annually or less frequently
  Supports:     flood_extent_assessment, cloudy_conditions, night_operations, emergency_response

  Description:  EUMETSAT operates Europe's meteorological satellites providing continuous atmospheric and meteorological monitoring.   Strengths:    15-minute precipitation estimates from geostationary Meteosat MSG SEVIRI; Hydrology SAF (H SAF) delivers operational near-real-time precipitation and soil moisture; Continuous 24/7 monitoring over Europe, Africa and the Atlantic

  [SENTINEL_1]
  Label:        Sentinel-1
  Organization: ESA / Copernicus
  Type:         satellite_mission
  Sensor:       SAR C-band (active microwave)
  Resolution:   5-40m (mode dependent; 10m in standard IW mode)
  Revisit:      6 days per satellite; ~3 days over Europe with constellation
  Access:       https://dataspace.copernicus.eu
  API:          https://catalogue.dataspace.copernicus.eu/stac/search
  Description:  Sentinel-1 is a polar-orbiting, all-weather, day-and-night C-band SAR mission. It is the primary satellite source for flood mapping in Europe and globally, providing backscatter imagery that clearly distinguishes open water from land regardless of cloud cover or lighting conditions.
  Strengths:    All-weather capability through cloud penetration; Day and night operations with active sensor; 10m spatial resolution in Interferometric Wide (IW) mode
  Limitations:  SAR imagery requires specialist processing skills (speckle, geometric correction); Flooded vegetation and urban areas produce complex backscatter signatures
  Supports:     flood_detection, flood_extent_assessment, cloudy_conditions, night_operations, emergency_response

  [SENTINEL_2]
  Label:        Sentinel-2
  Organization: ESA / Copernicus
  Type:         satellite_mission
  Sensor:       Multispectral MSI (13 bands, 443-2190nm)
  Resolution:   10m (visible/NIR), 20m (red-edge/SWIR), 60m (atmospheric)
  Revisit:      5 days at equator; 2-3 days at mid-latitudes (A+B constellation)
  Access:       https://dataspace.copernicus.eu
  API:          https://catalogue.dataspace.copernicus.eu/stac/search
  Description:  Sentinel-2 is a polar-orbiting multispectral optical mission optimised for land monitoring. It provides high-resolution surface reflectance imagery enabling accurate computation of spectral water indices (NDWI, MNDWI) for flood extent mapping under clear sky conditions.
  Strengths:    10m spatial resolution in visible and NIR bands; 290km swath width for large area coverage; Dedicated water detection bands (B3 Green, B8 NIR, B11/B12 SWIR)
  Limitations:  Completely blocked by cloud cover, which frequently accompanies flood events; No night-time capability
  Supports:     flood_detection, flood_extent_assessment, emergency_response

  [SENTINEL_3]
  Label:        Sentinel-3
  Organization: ESA / Copernicus / EUMETSAT
  Type:         satellite_mission
  Sensor:       OLCI (Ocean and Land Colour Instrument, 21 bands) + SLSTR (Sea and Land Surface Temperature Radiometer)
  Resolution:   300m (OLCI), 500m/1km (SLSTR)
  Revisit:      Less than 2 days globally (A+B tandem constellation)
  Access:       https://dataspace.copernicus.eu
  API:          https://catalogue.dataspace.copernicus.eu/stac/search
  Description:  Sentinel-3 is a multi-instrument ocean and land monitoring mission providing global coverage at medium resolution. Its OLCI sensor enables large-scale water body monitoring and flood extent estimation, while SLSTR provides surface temperature data useful for assessing flood-affected areas and evaporation dynamics.
  Strengths:    Near-daily global revisit (< 2 days with A+B tandem); Wide area coverage at 300m enabling continental-scale flood monitoring; 21 OLCI spectral bands optimised for water and land colour
  Limitations:  300m resolution too coarse for detailed urban or small river flood mapping; Optical instrument blocked by clouds
  Supports:     flood_detection, flood_extent_assessment, emergency_response

  Type:         satellite_mission
  Sensor:       TROPOMI (TROPOspheric Monitoring Instrument) — UV/VIS/NIR/SWIR spectrometer
  Resolution:   3.5 x 5.5 km (since August 2019)
  Revisit:      Daily global coverage
  Access:       https://dataspace.copernicus.eu
  API:          https://catalogue.dataspace.copernicus.eu/stac/search
  Description:  Sentinel-5P carries the TROPOMI instrument for daily global atmospheric monitoring at unprecedented spatial resolution. While primarily an atmospheric mission, it supports flood emergency response by detecting methane releases from flooded wetlands, monitoring NO2 and SO2 from industrial accidents accompanying floods, and assessing aerosol and cloud coverage to plan optical satellite observations.
  Strengths:    Daily global atmospheric coverage; Highest-resolution spaceborne atmospheric spectrometer for operational use; Detects secondary hazards co-occurring with floods (industrial accidents, gas leaks)
  Limitations:  Does not detect surface flooding directly; 3.5km pixel size far too coarse for flood extent mapping
  Supports:     cloudy_conditions, emergency_response
