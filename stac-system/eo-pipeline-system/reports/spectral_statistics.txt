ENGINEERING INTERPRETATION
==========================
1. Which band contains larger average values?
   Band B08 (Near-Infrared) contains larger average values than B04 (Red) in vegetated areas.
2. Why may vegetation behave differently in both bands?
   Chlorophyll absorbs red light (B04) for photosynthesis, while the cellular structure of leaves reflects near-infrared radiation (B08).
3. Why are these differences important for NDVI computation?
   The contrast between high absorption in Red and high reflection in NIR forms the mathematical base of NDVI to isolate vegetation.
