CONCLUSIONS

Which region appears easier to monitor using this collection?
From a pure catalog perspective, both regions have excellent orbital coverage. However, practically, the Kraków region may be slightly easier to monitor with optical imagery (Sentinel-2) because the Baltic region is highly susceptible to coastal cloud cover and sea fog, which degrades optical product usability.

Which operational risks may arise from uneven spatial coverage?
Uneven spatial coverage creates critical "blind spots". If an infrastructure failure or natural disaster occurs in an under-observed region, response teams will lack the necessary situational awareness. It can also bias AI/ML models if they are trained on regions with high data density but deployed in regions with sparse coverage.

Would an additional sensor type improve monitoring reliability?
Yes. For the Baltic coastal region, integrating a SAR (Synthetic Aperture Radar) collection like Sentinel-1 is strictly necessary. SAR sensors penetrate cloud cover and operate at night, completely mitigating the weather-related operational risks of the Baltic Sea.
